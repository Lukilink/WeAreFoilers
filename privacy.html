2. The short version

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no cookie banner needed.
  • Private foilers never show an address. The pin is moved randomly by up to 1.5 km when saved and drawn as a soft circle.
  • WhatsApp and email on a card are only visible to signed-in members. Everything on the card except name, home spot and disciplines is optional.
  • You can hide your card or delete your account at any time. Deleting removes your card, photo and account for good.

3. What we process and why

Visiting the site

When you open the site, your browser connects to our hosting provider GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA), which processes your IP address and technical data in server logs to deliver the page and keep it secure (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Map and place search

Map tiles are loaded from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org). Your browser sends your IP address to that server to receive the map. When you search for a place, the search term is sent to the Nominatim service of the OpenStreetMap Foundation (UK). “Use my location” only works after you allow it in your browser; the position is used to center the map or to look up the name of your town and is not stored as such (legitimate interest in a working map, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Account and card

For an account we store your email address and a hashed password. Your card contains what you enter: name or nickname, type (foiler, school, shop), home spot and optional travel spot, disciplines and levels, “open for” tags, gear, a short text, an optional photo and optional contact details (Instagram, WhatsApp, email, for schools and shops also website and opening hours). We use this data to show your card on the map (contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Who sees what: everyone sees your card without WhatsApp and email. Signed-in members additionally see WhatsApp and email if you added them. Your account email is never shown.

Account and card data are stored with Supabase (Supabase Inc.) in a data center in the EU (Ireland). Supabase acts as our processor under a data processing agreement.

Reports, approval and partner program

If someone reports a card, we store the reason, an optional note, the time and, if the reporter is signed in, their account ID. New cards may be checked by hand before they go live. If you ask for the partner badge, we store the link where you shared We Are Foilers and look at that page (legitimate interest in a safe and friendly community, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Schools and shops added automatically

Some schools and shops were added by us from publicly available information (name, town, website), so travellers can find them. Each of these cards is clearly marked. The business can claim the card or ask us to remove it at any time (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Emails

We send emails only when needed for your account (confirming your email, resetting your password) and, if something happens with your card, about that card.

Storage in your browser

To keep you signed in, a login token is stored in your browser's local storage. This is technically necessary. We do not use tracking cookies.

4. How long we keep data

Your card and account stay until you delete them. Reports are deleted together with the reported card. Server logs at our providers are kept for a short period for security reasons.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. Most of it you can do yourself: edit, hide or delete your card at any time. For anything else, contact us at mail@wearefoilers.com. You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in Spain the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

6. External links

Links to Instagram, WhatsApp or websites lead to services of other providers. Their privacy policies apply once you follow the link.
