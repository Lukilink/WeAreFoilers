1. What We Are Foilers is

A free, non-profit community map, run by Ola Vibes S.L. (see Imprint), that helps foilers find each other. We only make the introduction. We are not a party to anything members arrange with each other.

2. Who can join

You must be at least 18 years old. Each person, school or shop may have one account and one card. Schools and shops may describe their offer on their card, but the map is not an advertising board: no spam, no mass messages, no unrelated promotion.

3. Your card

  • Keep it honest. Use your own name or nickname and a real home spot.
  • Only upload photos you have the rights to and that show nothing inappropriate.
  • You decide which contact details you share. WhatsApp and email are only visible to signed-in members.
  • By publishing your card you allow us to show it on the map and in lists. You can hide or delete it at any time.

We may check new cards before they go live, and hide, block or remove cards that break these terms, that are reported for good reason, or that look fake. Anyone can report a card with the “Report” button.

Members who share We Are Foilers publicly (for example in their Instagram bio or on their website) can ask for the blue partner check. We give it at our discretion and may remove it, for example if the link is taken down.

7. Schools and shops added automatically

Some school and shop cards were created by us from public information and are marked as such. The business can claim the card from the card itself, or ask us to change or remove it.
