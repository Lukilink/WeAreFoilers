What this site does

We Are Foilers is a free, non-profit community map that helps foilers find each other. The cards on the map are created by the members themselves, apart from schools and shops that we added automatically from public information (marked as such on their card). We only make the introduction. Sessions, gear lending, rides and anything else are arranged directly between members, and Ola Vibes S.L. is not a party to those arrangements.

Content by members

Members are responsible for what they publish. If you notice unlawful or inappropriate content, use the “Report” button on the card or contact us. We remove affected content promptly once we know about it.

Trademarks

Brand names mentioned on cards (for example foil, board and wing brands) belong to their owners. We Are Foilers is independent and not affiliated with any brand.

Online dispute resolution

We are neither obliged nor willing to take part in dispute resolution proceedings before a consumer arbitration board.
